# Encode what is repeatable. Collaborate on what is novel.

**Major Principle 4 of Convergence** (working doctrine, v0.1)

When work is understood, repeated, and predictable enough to describe,
examine whether the organization is spending scarce expertise on it
transaction by transaction.

Repeated work is a **signal** to examine for reusable knowledge. It is
not proof that the work should be automated or encoded further.

**Make exceptional work exceptional again.**

## Encoding is broader than automation

Encoding can include documentation, standards, patterns, policies,
decision guidance, contracts, workflows, libraries, infrastructure
modules, software, automation, and AI context. Automation is only one
form.

The purpose of encoding expertise is **leverage**, not elimination of
specialists.

**Encoded expertise must remain challengeable.**

**Encoded expertise without living expertise eventually becomes legacy.**

Expertise must continue to evolve encoded knowledge when assumptions,
technologies, risks, or context change. Human realization is valid.
Encoding more is not inherently more mature.

See [Expertise encoding](../02-capabilities/expertise-encoding.md).

## What this principle does not mean

- Automate everything.
- Eliminate experts or human approval.
- Convert all judgment into software.
- Make every capability self-service.
- Treat documentation as inferior to code, or code as the only encoding.
- Treat repeated work as automatically ready to encode.

See [Major Principle 1](01-specialization-remains-silos-dont.md).
