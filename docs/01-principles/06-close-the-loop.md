# Close the loop from intent to learning.

**Major Principle 6 of Convergence** (working doctrine, v0.1)

The conceptual loop is intent → capability → experience / realization →
outcome → learning, with learning feeding back into the engineering
system. If that loop is fictional, Convergence is a diagram.

**Learning is evidence capable of changing what the engineering system
believes.**

Learning is not limited to telemetry. It may include evidence from
capability usage, failures, incidents, exceptions, abandonment, consumer
friction, cost, reliability, security findings, threat intelligence,
audits, regulatory change, technology change, customer feedback, expert
discovery, and external events.

**Learning informs change; it does not automatically mandate change.**

**Learning creates evidence for evolution; it does not guarantee that an
organization will act on that evidence.**

Politics and incentives are not additional lifecycle nodes. They can
block action on evidence. That limitation is real. It is parked as
operating-model and future-research work, not as a new stage in the
loop.

See [Learning](../02-capabilities/learning.md) and
[Open questions](../00-foundations/open-questions.md).

## What this principle does not mean

- Every incident becomes a new abstraction.
- Evidence always produces organizational change.
- Only production metrics count as learning.
- Infrastructure capabilities own business outcomes they only contribute
  to.

> Own the outcome you can control. Trace the outcomes you meaningfully
> contribute to.
