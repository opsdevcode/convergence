# Open questions

These topics emerged from hostile testing of the v0.1 conceptual core.
They are **parked**, not solved. Do not treat this list as a backlog of
required frameworks.

The v0.1 core (definitions, six Major Principles, design doctrine,
conceptual loop, capability mechanism) should stay frozen unless a
contradiction is found. Further work belongs here or in later roadmap
stages, not as new lifecycle nodes, maturity levels, or branded
sub-frameworks.

| Topic | Why it is open |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence and evidence independence | What counts as independent evidence is not specified. |
| Uncertainty representation | No confidence schema or risk score in v0.1. |
| Knowledge provenance | How encodings inherit and show their sources. |
| Reversibility | How to unwind encoded paths and compositions. |
| Consequence and blast radius | Named as concerns; not a measurement system. |
| Concentration risk | Reuse creates leverage and concentration together; no scoring. |
| Emergent composition risk | Safety of parts does not guarantee safety of composition. |
| Incentive compatibility | Acknowledged in the operating model; no budgeting model. |
| Long-term capability decay | Encoded expertise without living expertise becomes legacy. |
| Deprecation and lifecycle semantics | Not a required contract or graph schema. |
| Capability semantic drift | Names stay while meaning moves. |
| Trust across federation boundaries | Federation is a boundary condition, not a trust protocol. |
| System-level stewardship | Needed at the scope of the concern; implementation is open. |
| Evolution over long organizational time | Learning does not guarantee action. |

Related later stages on the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) remain placeholders
(maturity characteristics, patterns, reference architecture). They must
not silently become the missing frameworks above.
