# Scaling organization

Illustrative: hundreds of engineers; Product, Platform, SRE, Security,
Data, Cloud or Infrastructure exist as functions.

Recurring organizational interactions become **explicit capabilities**
(still not necessarily APIs): for example `DeployApplication`,
`ProvideRelationalStorage`, `EstablishIdentity` (or
`EstablishServiceIdentity`), `PublishEvent`, `CreateDataPipeline`,
`ExposePublicEndpoint`.

An internal platform **may** participate in experience or realization.
It does not become the definition of Convergence. A platform that is
only a ticket router has not changed the model.

Capability ownership can sit with a platform group for a composite such
as `DeployApplication` while domain authority for identity, security,
and reliability stays with those specialties.
[Shared contribution does not require shared accountability.](../02-capabilities/ownership-and-authority.md)

Organizational independence is a spectrum: some paths are usable from a
pipeline; exceptions still reach specialists without pretending every
intent is self-service.
