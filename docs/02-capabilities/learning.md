# Learning

**Learning is evidence produced by capability use and outcomes that
changes the engineering system.**

Learning may come from usage, failures, exceptions, consumer friction,
incidents, cost, reliability behavior, security findings, business
results, human feedback, and repeated "novel" requests.

Learning may change capability definitions, contracts, experiences,
realizations, composition, policy, and ownership.

## Example

A security architect repeatedly handles the same supposedly novel
request. The organization learns the problem is becoming repeatable.
Some of that judgment may then be represented through standards, policy,
documented patterns, tooling, automation, or another reusable mechanism.

The capability existed before this encoding. What changed was its
[realization](realization.md) and the leverage of the expertise behind
it.

See [Expertise encoding](expertise-encoding.md).
