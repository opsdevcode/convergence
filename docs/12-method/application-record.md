# Application record

A thinking aid for one pass of the
[initial application method](README.md). A page of notes is enough.

It is **not** a review-board deliverable, compliance evidence, or a
required governance artifact.

Use this shape. Leave sections **unused**, **not applicable**, or
**unknown** where that is honest. Do not turn an unknown into an
investigation unless it would block a *safe* decision.

Do not add a second document to look complete.

## Intent / outcome

- Class of intent:
- Outcome that counts:
- Why this method applies (trigger), or N/A:
- Who owns the result of this analysis:

## Current trace

Ordered interactions from recent instances (not the org chart). For
each, as useful: decides / authority / expertise / waits on / same
answer every time? / why it waits (hypothesis). Unknown is allowed.

## Meaningful waits and dependencies

What actually delays the outcome. Include **unknown**.

## Diagnostics used

When using this method: experience vs realization; accidental vs
intentional — unless a section is N/A.

Conditional (only if triggered): capability name; encode vs novel;
authority vs execution; composition seam; organizational independence.

## Intentional constraints

What must be preserved: judgment, governance, safety, authority, risk
control. Who holds each. Unknown/N/A if not yet in play.

## System problem

One or two sentences. The org chart is not the diagnosis. Or N/A if
the pass stops before diagnose.

## Proposed intervention

Including **change nothing** and **explicitly deferred**, with reason.

## Wrong-success condition

**What could look like success while the engineering system did not
actually improve?**

## Evidence to observe

What you will look at after this pass (qualitative is allowed), or none
if the pass ends. Learning informs change; it does not mandate it.
