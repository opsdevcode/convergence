# Original thesis reconciliation

*Audit trail for a course correction. Not a novelty study. Not a naming
brief. Not a product brief.*

**Decision.** Restore the **thesis** as the center of identity. Keep
research as **guardrails** (honesty, no invention claims, no mature-
methodology claim). Do **not** shrink the project to the leftover
“bundled diagnostic” from methodology-distinctiveness analysis.

---

## 1. Reconstructed thesis (defensible)

Modern software delivery needs deep specialization. The problem is not
that specialists exist. The problem is when the **organizational
boundaries** that contain that expertise become the **interface of
routine delivery**: tickets, queues, approvals, meetings, handoffs,
ownership lookup, tribal routing. Delivery then copies the org chart.
Convergence is the developing response: keep specialization, domain
authority, human judgment, ownership, accountability, provenance, and
intentional governance; **converge delivery** so routine intent is
satisfied through coherent **capabilities** rather than an org walk.
Encode what is repeatable; collaborate on what is novel. Reduce
unnecessary coordination; preserve collaboration. Organizational
structure should not be the software delivery API.

Shorthand that still holds: “Specialization remains. Silos don’t.”
“Convergence isn’t the convergence of expertise. It’s the convergence of
delivery.” “One product. Many specialties. One engineering system.”

## 2. Research ledger (must preserve)

**Strengthened**

- Specialization as delivery architecture is an **industry-recognizable**
  problem (coupling, blocking deps, wait, local vs system).
- Experience vs realization, capability vs team/API, human realization,
  authority ≠ execution, accidental vs intentional friction remain
  **useful** even when not novel.
- Team Topologies, Platform Engineering, DORA, Lean, SRE, systems
  engineering, POM are **real neighbors**.
- Abstraction cost and cross-specialty audience are real.
- Frozen vs applied boundary matters (planning must not become theory).

**Corrected**

- Component ideas have **substantial prior art**; do not claim invention.
- TT is complementary, not “just reorg.” PE is not obsolete.
- AI exposes cost; it does not **require** or **prove** Convergence.
- “Evolution of the industry” / movement language overclaimed.
- Planning as a new ontology was overclaim; capability-oriented planning
  is **not** a canonical novelty.
- Completion-vs-contract is **application**, not new theory.
- A **combination** of established practices can reproduce **many
  designs**.
- No **single named** methodology was found to be a drop-in equivalent
  (methodology-distinctiveness). That does **not** forbid developing
  **this** work as an emerging methodology.

**Killed (stay dead)**

- New engineering discipline / specialty / Convergence function.
- Replacement of DevOps, PE, TT, SRE.
- Invented capabilities / capability-oriented planning as distinct
  physics.
- Planning objects / capability roadmaps as canonical.
- Path-owner role.
- AI requires this model.
- Mature/validated methodology (not yet).
- Unique because of vocabulary.
- Portal/IDP/catalog as the work.
- Maximum self-service / automate everything.
- Productization of Convergence as an OpsDevCode runtime.

**Foundational (unchanged by this correction)**

Six principles; conceptual loop; capability definition (ability, not
API); experience vs realization; authority model; composition without
domain takeover; encode/collaborate; intentional friction.
