# Convergence v0.9.0-research.2 — Research Snapshot

**Interim research snapshot**

This snapshot records research progress. It does not represent a new
canonical methodology version.

Convergence remains an emerging methodology for designing software
delivery as a coherent engineering system rather than a sequence of
organizational handoffs. The conceptual core is unchanged from
`v0.9.0-research.1`.

This is not a validated or mature methodology, industry standard,
certification, production-ready body of work, or claim of demonstrated
effectiveness. It is not a new identity for Convergence and not a
replacement for existing engineering disciplines.

## What changed

Since `v0.9.0-research.1` (`760622e`), `origin/main` gained one merged
commit:

- `42c6d1e` — `research: examine handoff properties and bounded decisions (#24)`

That commit adds only
`drafts/research/handoff-properties-and-bounded-decisions.md`.

This snapshot also adds non-canonical research-status and index notes so
the epistemic state is visible in the tree. Canonical definition,
principles, conceptual model, capabilities, application method, and
terminology are not revised.

## What we learned

The added research note treats organizational handoffs as possibly
carrying engineering properties as well as transferring work. It keeps
that question as a hypothesis. It does not promote a new principle.

Paper analysis was not sufficient to add conceptual-model nodes, an
evidence taxonomy, or a universal decision schema. Several distinctions
remain research-only and may still collapse into existing
accidental-versus-intentional friction, Observation, and
authority-versus-execution material.

## What did not change

- Canonical definition of Convergence and Converged Engineering
- Six Major Principles
- Conceptual model
- Capability definitions
- Initial five-move application method
- Canonical terminology
- Architecture doctrine
- Frozen practitioner-validation v1 inputs
- Roadmap stage (still v0.9 community review)

## What remains research

- Handoff-property analysis and property-versus-mechanism wording
- Evidence lenses (not a taxonomy)

## Validation status

Independent practitioner validation is not included as results. Protocol
v1 and its harness remain frozen and unexecuted. A GitHub Release, if
published from this snapshot, identifies a historical research state. It
is not empirical evidence.

## Next research step

Keep additional inquiry in research drafts unless an RFC can show that
the frozen core is contradicted. Do not promote research vocabulary.
Execute practitioner-validation v1 separately if empirical evidence is
the next goal.

## Interpretation

A GitHub Release in this repository identifies a meaningful snapshot of
the evolving body of work. Version numbers identify research and
methodology states. They do not imply software compatibility or
maturity beyond the documents themselves.

`v0.9.0-research.2` is the second interim research snapshot against the
v0.9 baseline. It is not v0.9.1, v0.10, or v1.0.
