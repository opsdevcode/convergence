## Session sequence

1. Assign a practitioner identifier.
2. Record pre-session metadata allowed by the protocol.
3. Give the practitioner **only** `practitioner-packet.md`.
4. The practitioner completes the analysis independently.
5. The researcher provides only permitted procedural clarification if needed.
6. Record every question, response, interruption, deviation, and coaching
   event.
7. Freeze the original practitioner analysis.
8. Preserve the frozen analysis unchanged.
9. Ask the 12 post-analysis questions in exact protocol order.
10. Perform the source-recognition check.
11. Only after those steps may provenance or held-back retrospective material
    be revealed or compared.
12. Preserve post-analysis and retrospective material separately from the
    frozen original analysis.

Cross-participant comparison occurs only after all four original analyses are
frozen.

## Do not

- Do not give the practitioner any artifact other than
  `practitioner-packet.md` before the analysis is frozen.
- Do not reveal another participant's analysis, provenance, held-back
  retrospective evidence, author diagnosis, suggested intervention, or whether
  the reasoning is correct.
- Do not permit external incident research or AI-generated analysis.
- Do not provide substantive coaching. Record every deviation and do not
  silently discard a contaminated result.
- Do not edit the frozen original analysis after it is frozen.
